## ⚡ How to Enable GitHub Pages (1-Minute Setup)
1. Go to your GitHub repository: **[https://github.com/14-Nishanth/flyash](https://github.com/14-Nishanth/flyash)**
2. Click **Settings** (tab at the top) $\longrightarrow$ Click **Pages** (on the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment** $\longrightarrow$ **Source**, select **`GitHub Actions`**.
4. That's it! GitHub Actions will automatically deploy your live web app to **`https://14-nishanth.github.io/flyash/`**.

## ✨ Key Features & Architecture
* **Executive Plant Dashboard:** Real-time production telemetry, dispatches, receivables vs. payables, and accurate active raw inventory.
* **1-Click WhatsApp Delivery Challans:** Instantly generate and share delivery receipts with truck drivers & customers.
* **Live Reactive Piece-Rate Calculator:** Dynamically computes Gross Pcs, Breakage Wastage Cut, Net Payable Units, and Gang Wage distribution as you type the tray count.
* **Parties & Double-Entry Ledgers:** Customer & Supplier accounts with printable statement sheets and payment logging.
* **Daily Attendance Register & Consolidated Wage Sheet:** Worker attendance tracking and combined salary calculation.
* **Supabase PostgreSQL Cloud Integration:** Connect your Supabase project in **Settings** to sync data live across all mobile devices (with offline-first local persistence).
